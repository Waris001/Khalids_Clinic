PATIENT INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some of the most common questions about consultations, surgical procedures and patient care.

Please bring your previous medical reports, prescriptions, diagnostic reports and a list of any medications you are currently taking. This helps the doctor understand your medical history and provide appropriate guidance.

We recommend scheduling an appointment in advance so that adequate time can be reserved for your consultation. Walk-in consultations may also be accommodated depending on availability.

The clinic provides consultation and surgical care for a range of conditions. Depending on the patient's condition, procedures may include general surgery, laparoscopic procedures, hernia surgery, gallbladder surgery and other surgical treatments.

Recovery time varies depending on the type of procedure, the patient's overall health and the complexity of the condition. During your consultation, the doctor will explain the expected recovery period and aftercare instructions.

You can book a consultation by calling the clinic directly or by using the online appointment form on our website.
