Meet Your Surgeon

Dr. Khalid Noman

M.B.B.S., M.S., F.I.A.G.E.S.
Senior Consultant at Chandan Hospital, Lucknow

Dr. Khalid Noman is a Laparoscopic and General Surgeon with a focused interest in advanced laparoscopic and gastrointestinal surgery. His approach combines surgical expertise with careful clinical evaluation and a strong emphasis on the individual needs of every patient.

As a specialist in Advanced Laparoscopy and Gastrointestinal Surgery, Dr. Khalid provides surgical consultation and treatment across a broad range of abdominal and gastrointestinal conditions.

His philosophy is simple — understand the patient, explain the condition clearly, discuss the available treatment options and provide care with precision, compassion and responsibility.

Our Approach

Surgery with Precision & Compassion

Every surgical condition is different, and every patient brings a different set of concerns, expectations and needs.

At Dr. Khalid's practice, consultation begins with listening. The goal is to understand the patient's symptoms and medical condition, explain the diagnosis in clear language and help the patient make an informed decision about treatment.
